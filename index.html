FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before beginning your interior design journey with LuxeNest.

Still have questions? Let's talk ↗
What type of projects do you take on? +

We work across residential interiors, apartments, villas, penthouses, individual rooms, kitchens and complete turnkey projects.

How does the design process begin? +

Every project begins with a consultation. We learn about your space, lifestyle, requirements, aesthetic preferences and goals before developing the design direction.

Do you handle execution as well? +

Yes. For turnkey projects, our team can coordinate execution, materials, vendors, craftsmanship and final styling.

Can I hire LuxeNest for one room? +

Absolutely. We also work on focused spaces such as bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, dining spaces and home offices.

How long does a project usually take? +

Timelines depend on the size and scope of the project. During the consultation, we provide a realistic timeline based on your requirements.
